SeaSniper

A gamified spearfishing learning journey that redirects apex predator hunters toward restoration-positive targets — giving spearfishers a sporting chance to target something else and become ocean protectors.

5% to MVP
Concept Stage
The Problem & Solution

Turning hunters into ocean protectors

Problems We're Solving
1

Spearfishers are highly motivated ocean enthusiasts who often target apex predators (snapper, crayfish) — the very animals needed to control kina

2

People are looking for new ocean experiences — the traditional spearfishing journey lacks structure, education, and connection to conservation outcomes

3

The spearfishing community is large and passionate — but largely untapped as a force for ocean restoration

How SeaSniper Solves It
1

Gamifies the learning journey of spearfishing — levelling up targets from kina and invasive species toward sustainable harvest as skills grow

2

Underwater headset technology investigation — immersive in-water experiences that make restoration targets as exciting as apex predators

3

Community of spearos who log kina removals, share data with Pingo, and earn restoration credits for their ocean contribution

Roadmap

From concept to community

1

Investigate Gameplay

Explore game mechanics that make kina targeting as rewarding as apex predator hunting. User research with NZ spearfishing community.

2

Underwater Headset

Explore waterproof AR/VR headset options for in-water gamification. Current focus: waterproofing first — full underwater deployment later.

3

Community Platform

Spearo community logging kina removals, sharing data with Pingo, and earning restoration leaderboard positions. Integration with Kelp Helpers impact tracking.

4

Conservation Partnership

Formal partnerships with LegaSea, spearfishing clubs, and fishing associations — turning the recreational fishing community into a restoration army.
