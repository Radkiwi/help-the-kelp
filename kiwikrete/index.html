The Problem & Solution

Turning ocean waste into ocean investment

Problems We're Solving
1

Kina shells have limited value as waste — not enough incentive for divers to collect them at scale

2

Low market awareness of kina barrens means the problem is invisible to most consumers

3

Conventional concrete and engineered stone materials destroy ecosystems — no ocean benefit

How KiwiKrete Solves It
1

Increases the value of kina shell waste — target: $100/tonne — making collection economically worthwhile

2

Beautiful physical products put the kina barren story into homes, offices, and public spaces — making it tangible

3

KinaKrete restores ocean biodiversity and adds value to "waste" — kina are produced by ocean nutrients, returned as products
