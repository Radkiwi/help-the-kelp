Why It Matters

Kelp forests are the rainforests of the sea

🐟

Biodiversity

A single kelp forest supports hundreds of species — fish, invertebrates, marine mammals. Lose the kelp, lose the ecosystem.

🌡️

Carbon Sink

Kelp absorbs CO₂ up to 20x faster than land forests per hectare, locking carbon into the deep ocean floor.

🌊

Coastal Protection

Dense kelp canopies reduce wave energy, protecting coastlines from erosion and buffering storm surge.

⚠️

Under Threat

Ocean warming and urchin barrens have wiped out over 90% of kelp in some regions. The forests that remain are fighting to survive.
